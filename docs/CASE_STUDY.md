# Case study — A desk can pass VaR backtesting and still fail P&L attribution

## Executive finding

The deterministic reference run uses 1,800 synthetic business-day observations from 2016-01-04 through 2022-11-25. It creates four hypothetical trading desks and compares full-revaluation hypothetical P&L (HPL) with independently constructed risk-theoretical P&L (RTPL).

The equity-options desk intentionally contains short at-the-money calls and puts plus a simplified short implied-variance exposure. Its delta-only risk model sees the S&P 500 spot move but omits volatility and curvature risk.

| Equity-options desk, latest 250 observations | Delta-only RTPL | RTPL with gamma + vega |
|---|---:|---:|
| Spearman rank correlation | 0.3754 | 0.9993 |
| KS empirical-CDF distance | 0.3960 | 0.0240 |
| PLA zone | **Red** | **Green** |
| Mean absolute HPL − RTPL residual | USD 5.63m | USD 0.58m |
| Mean absolute residual reduction | — | **89.7%** |

The same delta-only model records **3 VaR exceptions in 250 observations at 99%**, which is in the green backtesting zone. This is the point of the case: a low exception count does not prove that the model explains the desk's economic P&L. Backtesting and PLA answer different questions.

## Desks and model-risk hypotheses

| Desk | Hypothetical positions | Risk factors / challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Equity Options | Short ATM SPX-style calls and puts; simplified variance-swap proxy | Delta-only risk omits vega and gamma |
| FX Options | Long EUR/USD option and spot control | Delta-only approximation omits FX volatility and curvature |
| Rates | Long 2Y and short 10Y zero-coupon proxies | First-order approximation omits bond convexity |
| Commodities | Long WTI Cushing and short Europe Brent spot-linked linear proxies (not futures) | Exact linear delta explain; separate WTI/Brent benchmark and basis risk |

All quantities and instruments are fictitious. The variance-swap mark is a deliberately simplified implied-variance proxy, not a complete market-standard variance-swap valuation.

## HPL and constant-maturity test-book convention

HPL is generated by full repricing of the prior-close positions using current-close market data. The positions are static during each daily close-to-close interval. After that interval is marked, the hypothetical test book is re-established at its declared constant maturity for the following day. This avoids the invalid assumption that a three- or six-month option was held unchanged for the entire seven-year sample.

RTPL is generated independently from previous-close sensitivities. The delta-only model uses the spot / yield risk factors but deliberately omits option volatility and curvature. The expanded model adds volatility sensitivity and second-order curvature. It does not claim to be a complete production Greeks library.

## Backtesting and PLA

For the most recent 250 aligned observations:
- Backtesting compares one-day HPL loss against a rolling VaR forecast built from the previous 250 observations. Actual P&L (APL) is not available.
- PLA compares the HPL and RTPL distributions using Spearman rank correlation and empirical CDF distance / two-sample KS statistic.
- The implemented PLA zones are Green: Spearman > 0.80 and KS < 0.09; Red: Spearman < 0.70 or KS > 0.12; otherwise Amber.

## Capital and stress outputs

The reference synthetic run also produces:
- 97.5% historical ES and 10-day liquidity-adjusted ES across 10/20/40/60/120-day liquidity horizons.
- A selected 250-day high-tail stress window.
- Reduced-factor selection tested against the latest 60 business-day endpoints using a 250-day estimation window, with average reduced/full ES coverage required to be at least 75%.
- Constrained / unconstrained IMCC aggregation with rho = 0.5.
- RFET evidence status, deliberately not certified from closing-price-only evidence.

The generated JSON / CSV files contain the exact run outputs. The stress and capital figures remain illustrative because the source history is synthetic and does not extend back to 2007.

## Scope and guardrails

- No real bank portfolio, Actual P&L, transaction-level quote ledger or supervisory approval is available.
- Public close series are not automatically qualifying RFET observations.
- The public S&P 500 daily series and synthetic reference history do not provide the full Basel stress history back to 2007.
- This is not a complete IMA implementation, regulatory capital number, or production valuation system.
